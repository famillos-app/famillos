How it works

Set up in ten minutes

📱

Build your list

Open the Famillos app on your phone or the Chrome extension on your laptop. Save products from any shop and add why you love each one.

⭐

Publish it

Choose “Creator list”, add your photo and bio, and publish. Your page updates whenever you change the list.

🔗

Share everywhere

Put the link in your bio, stories and captions. Followers can shop straight from your page. They don't need the app.

Earning

Two ways to earn

Pick per product, and mix both on one list.

🔗 Use your own links

Already in Amazon Associates, LTK, Awin or a brand programme? Paste your link on any product. Followers go straight through it.

  • Commission goes to your own account
  • Works with any programme you belong to

🤝 Use Famillos partner links

No programme yet? Leave the link field empty and Famillos adds its partner links. Clicks are tracked to your page.

  • Commission shared with you each month (terms in your creator agreement)
  • Covers thousands of shops through our affiliate networks

Every creator page is automatically labelled “Contains affiliate links · #ad”, in line with UK ASA/CMA and US FTC guidance. You remain responsible for disclosing affiliate links in your own posts too.
