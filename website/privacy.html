Famillos

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Famillos (“we”, “us”), available at famillos.com as a Chrome extension and a web app, helps parents and carers keep baby shopping lists, track or split costs, share tasks and find local activities. This policy explains what data the extension and this website handle, and why. Questions: privacy@famillos.com.

The short version

  • Your lists, expenses and tasks are stored on your device. If you turn on partner sync, they are also stored in our database so your partner's browser can see them.
  • We only read a web page when you open the Famillos panel or choose “Save to Famillos”, and (only if you allow product detection) on order-confirmation pages to offer adding the total to your expenses.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising profiles, or share it with data brokers.
  • We do not collect your browsing history.

Registry guests

You don't need an account to reserve a gift. We create a random anonymous ID in your browser so you can undo your reservation later; it isn't linked to your identity. The name and message you type are shared only with the registry owner.

Creator lists and affiliate links

Creator lists are public. Links on registry and creator pages may be affiliate links (Amazon Associates, Awin, Skimlinks or Sovrn, or the creator's own programme). When you click one, that network may set a cookie to credit the referral. Pages that contain affiliate links say so.

Sharing by message

When you use “Send” to share a list, balance or to-dos by WhatsApp, text or email, Famillos just opens that app with the text pre-filled. We don't see or store those messages.

Service providers

Partner sync uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes data on our behalf under Google's terms. Activity searches use OpenStreetMap services (Overpass API and Nominatim/Photon geocoding), which receive the place name you type and the search area — not your identity. If we enable Google Places, the same search details go to Google. The website only loads Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel after you accept the cookie banner. The extension itself contains no analytics or ad trackers.

Affiliate links

Some deals in the Deals tab are affiliate links and are labelled “Affiliate”. When you click one, the retailer or affiliate network may set a cookie to credit us for the referral. We never insert, replace or modify affiliate links or cookies on pages you browse on your own.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide Famillos's user-facing features, is not transferred to third parties except as described above, and is never used for advertising, credit-worthiness or lending decisions.

Your choices

  • Export everything any time: Settings → Your data → Export backup.
  • Delete local data: Settings → Reset everything, or uninstall the extension.
  • Delete your account and synced data: email us from your account address and we'll erase it within 30 days.
  • Turn off page reading: Settings → Product detection → Turn off.

Children

Famillos is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. The baby name field is optional and can be a nickname.

Security & retention

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Synced family data is only readable by the two signed-in members of that family, enforced by database security rules. Deleted items are fully purged from sync after 30 days. Inactive accounts may be deleted after 24 months.

Changes

We'll update the date above and, for significant changes, show a notice in the extension.
