Questions

Good to know

Is Famillos really free?

Yes. Lists, budgets, cost splitting, tasks, reminders and sync are free. We keep the lights on through clearly labelled affiliate links on some deals — you never pay more.

Does anyone else need to install it?

No. Famillos works on its own, and you can send a co-parent, grandparent or friend any list, the cost split or their to-dos by WhatsApp, text or email. If they do install it, you can link up with a family code and everything syncs live.

What can Famillos see when I browse?

Only the product page you're on, and only when you open the panel or right-click to save. If you turn on order detection, it also reads checkout confirmation pages to offer adding the total to your expenses. We never sell your data. Read the privacy policy.

Which countries and stores does it work with?

Famillos is built for families in the UK, Ireland, Europe and the US — prices show in £, € or $, and deals and activities are matched to your country. Clipping works on almost any online shop (Amazon, John Lewis, Boots, Argos, Target, Walmart, bol.com, dm, Vertbaudet, Etsy and thousands more). If something isn't detected, you can type it in.

Do you use affiliate links?

Some deals in the Deals tab are affiliate links, and they're labelled “Affiliate”. If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We never swap or insert affiliate links on pages you browse.

Is there a phone app?

Yes — open the Famillos web app on your phone and choose “Add to Home Screen”. It works on iPhone and Android. The Chrome extension needs Chrome 116+ on Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS; Edge and Brave support is coming.

How do the activity listings work?

We search open map data from OpenStreetMap (and optionally Google) around the area you choose. We only store a rough area — never your exact address. Details like opening hours can change, so check with the venue before you go.
